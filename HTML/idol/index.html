Description

Early life and education

May Thu Aung was born and raised in Yangon, Myanmar, where she attended Basic Education High School No. 2 Lanmadaw and later pursued her higher education, graduating with a degree in English from the University of Distance Education, Yangon in 2011.

Career

May, also known as May Thu Aung, is a well-known Burmese pop singer, actress, and model who has built a successful career in Myanmar's entertainment industry. Born and raised in Yangon, she initially gained public recognition through beauty pageants before transitioning into acting and music. She officially entered the music scene in 2009 by participating in group albums, and later achieved major milestone success with her hit solo albums such as 'Marry Me?' and 'Htwat Pauk,' alongside numerous popular singles and award-winning movie theme songs. Beyond her musical achievements, she maintains a strong presence across social media platforms, connecting closely with her fans through her lifestyle content, performances, and ongoing artistic projects.
